Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

This site is a memorial for Izzy Penston. It exists to celebrate their life and give people a place to share memories. We're not a company — we're a family. We collect the minimum information needed to run the site, and we treat it with care.

What we collect

When you submit a memory through the form on this site, we collect your name, your relationship to Izzy (optional), your email address, the text of your memory, and any photos you choose to include. Nothing is collected passively — no tracking pixels, no analytics cookies, no ad networks.

How we use it

We review every submission before it appears on the site. Your email address is only used if we need to reach you about your submission — it's never displayed publicly and never shared. Approved memories (name, relationship, and memory text) are published on izzypenston.com. Submitted photos may also be published alongside the memory.

Where data is stored

Submissions are stored as issues in a private GitHub repository managed by the Penston family. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that storage. Uploaded photos are committed to the site's repository and served via GitHub Pages.

Cloudflare

This site is served through Cloudflare, which acts as our CDN and DNS provider. The memory submission form also uses Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-friendly alternative to traditional CAPTCHAs, to help prevent spam. Turnstile does not use tracking cookies or show ads. Cloudflare's handling of data is described in their privacy policy.

Data removal

If you'd like your submitted memory or photos removed from the site, just email us at memories@izzypenston.com and we'll take care of it.
